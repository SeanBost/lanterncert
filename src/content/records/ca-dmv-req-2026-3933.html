The California Department of Motor Vehicles acknowledges receipt of your request, requesting records pursuant to the California Public Records Act (Government Code section 7920.000 et seq.).

“I request records sufficient to show, for the Class M1 and M2 motorcycle knowledge tests currently administered: (1) the number of scored items on each test form, (2) the number of correct responses required to pass, and (3) the number of alternate forms in use.

I am not requesting test items, scoring keys, or other examination data exempt under Gov. Code § 7929.605, and I ask that any responsive record be redacted rather than withheld in full if it contains such material.”

The Department has closed this Public Records Act request due to being unable to locate exact records with the information you provided.

However, The Department can share that the Motorcycle written testing is comprised of 25 questions, and an applicant will automatically fail if 5 questions are deemed incorrect.

If you require more information, please resubmit a new Public Records Act request and include specific identifying information on what records are being requested.
